## Screen and journey coverage

Every destination in the [Desk index](pages/README.md) and [portal index](portal/README.md) links its applicable acceptance IDs. The [flow index](flows/README.md) covers identity, registration, renewal, requests, publication, bidding, evaluation, award, contract/order, fulfilment/finance, automation and migration/offboarding. All are required design surfaces; hiding a destination is not an implementation substitute.

The structural validator confirms every defined requirement/acceptance is referenced by tasks and traceability, task dependencies exist without cycles and all expected screens/flows exist. Semantic review still checks authority, time, stage release, native ownership and failure consistency across owners.
