# Data model and record lifecycles

Owner: PROC-R01, PROC-R03–PROC-R26, PROC-R28. This file owns persisted fields, relationships and states. Policy details remain in their linked subject owners. All `PROC` names below are future owned DocTypes, not existing implementation.

## Common conventions

Use Frappe Link, Dynamic Link, Data, Int, Check, Date, Datetime, Currency, Percent, Select, JSON and Table fields with native precision. Names below are field names; `?` means optional, otherwise required at the relevant lifecycle gate. Frappe `name`, `owner`, creation/modified metadata are implicit. Business roots include `revision:Int`, `status:Select`, initiating actor, and audit references. Company-owned records include `company:Link Company`; global organisation/membership records do not invent a single company owner.

Store event times as domain UTC instants through a tested Frappe timezone conversion; public ISO timestamps include offset. Store legal dates as Date and derive expiry instants in the policy timezone. Currency values always accompany currency and precision. Binary files remain private storage objects, referenced by owned evidence IDs; do not put unrestricted Attach URLs on envelope parents.

Definitions and submissions are immutable once published/submitted, except service-managed lifecycle pointers/status. Use new revisions instead of mutable JSON. Disable generic write/import on managed records, controller-check fields even if metadata marks them read-only, and avoid standard Version capture of secret/envelope bodies. Snapshot hashes use canonical serialisation of typed non-secret values and file checksums; secret references are opaque IDs.

## Identity and company approval

**PROC Vendor Organisation:** `legal_name:Data`, `trading_name?:Data`, `country:Link Country`, `registration_identifier:Data`, `identity_key:Data unique`, `identity_status:Select Pending/Verified/Disputed/Closed`, `supplier?:Link Supplier`, `profile_revision:Int`, `primary_contact?:Link Contact`, `address?:Link Address`, categories/coverage/work-types child tables, `closure_reason?`. `identity_key` is a versioned country-normalised identifier. Supplier link is unique when populated, enforced through a separate Supplier-link registry to avoid database-dependent nullable uniqueness assumptions. Potential matches without a reliable identifier use a restricted duplicate case, not guessed identity keys.

**PROC Supplier Link Registry:** organisation and Supplier, each individually unique, with reviewed decision/audit reference. Populate only through activation/reconciliation; a missing optional Supplier link does not become a nullable uniqueness loophole.

**PROC Organisation Claim:** claimant User, proposed country/identifier/name, identity evidence references, matched organisation? (internal only), reviewer, decision/reason, state Pending verification/Under review/Resolved/Rejected. Only the claimant's safe projection is exposed until membership is granted.

**PROC Vendor Membership:** organisation, user, role Owner/Contributor, capability child rows (Bid Submitter, Billing, Bank Maintainer), status Invited/Active/Revoked, invitation token digest/expiry?, invited_by, verified_at?, revoked_at?. Unique `(organisation,user)`. Invitation acceptance cannot change organisation or grantor-selected capabilities. At least one active Owner remains unless an independently approved recovery/closure operation is running.

**PROC Company Approval:** company, organisation, `supplier?` (required at activation), `status` Unverified/Active/Compliance Hold/Suspended/Closed, approved_at?, approval_submission?, company-specific reason, and category rows containing category, state, validity and last decision. Unique `(company,organisation)`. An unverified approval shell may precede Supplier creation; activation supplies the reviewed link atomically. Category state is managed; effective expiry is evaluated live, not trusted from a cached status.

**PROC Evidence Grant:** evidence revision, granting organisation/actor, receiving company, purpose, granted_at, revoked_at?. Grants authorise reuse, not independent acceptance.

**PROC Bank Change:** company, organisation, proposed protected account fields/reference, masked display, account fingerprint, proof evidence, requested_by, old_verified_bank_reference?, verifier?, verification_method?, verified_at?, decision_reason?, status Draft/Submitted/Verifying/Approved/Rejected/Cancelled, native_bank_account?. Preserve previous verified records and account revision; do not overwrite submitted payment instructions.

**PROC Duplicate Case:** candidate claims/organisations/Suppliers, restricted match reasons, resolution Link/Merge rejected/Distinct/Needs evidence, independent resolver, audit. Merges require a separate previewed mapping operation preserving legal identity and source history.

## Configuration, requirements and review

**PROC Procurement Category:** native tree master with stable code, translated label, parent, permitted work types, optional Item Group mappings and Active/Retired state. **PROC Service Region:** native tree master with stable code, label, parent, Country and Active/Retired state. Use these identifiers in applications, tenders and subscriptions rather than fuzzy free-text matching. A selected parent covers its descendants; a vendor's selected region covers tender regions equal to or below it. Category matching likewise honours selected parent coverage, subject to company-approved category scope. Tender-required category conjunction is explicitly published. Retiring a master prevents new selection without rewriting historical versions.

**PROC Company Policy:** company, version, effective_from, timezone, allowed categories/work types, sourcing/authority bands, review/routing stages, budget policy, matching/complaint settings, invoice tolerances, retention-policy reference, delegated-authority references, readiness state Draft/Published/Retired. Unique `(company,version)` and one managed current-policy pointer per company. Bands/stages are child rows with stable keys. Published policy JSON/hash is immutable; resolve semantics in [policy](PROCUREMENT_POLICY.md).

**PROC Requirement Pack:** company, stable pack_key, purpose Registration/Renewal/Tender, selectors (country/category/work type), current_published_version?. **PROC Requirement Version:** pack, version, effective dates, requirements child rows, stage definitions, hash, published_by/at, status Draft/Published/Retired; unique `(pack,version)`. A requirement row has stable key, section/order, type, translated label/help, allowed options, required flag, condition JSON, validation JSON, sensitivity, document policy, verification method, review-stage key and sharing permission. Version contents are not editable after publication.

**PROC Vendor Application:** company, organisation, kind Initial/Renewal/Remediation, previous_application?, resolved version-reference rows/hash, selected categories/work types/coverage, current draft revision, latest_submission?, assigned_stage?, assigned_reviewer?, status Draft/Submitted/Under review/Needs correction/Approved/Rejected/Withdrawn. A locked application head/active-application key prevents concurrent active applications for the same company/organisation/kind; an independent remediation campaign can coexist with an initial/renewal flow and declares affected categories explicitly.

**PROC Application Submission:** application, sequence, resolved versions/hash, answers snapshot, evidence revision references, declaration/notice acceptances, submitted_by/at, response_hash, supersedes?. Unique `(application,sequence)`. Answers use typed child responses: requirement key, value type, typed value or bounded canonical JSON for multiselect, sensitivity and evidence reference; protected secrets are referenced, never copied into public snapshots.

**PROC Evidence:** organisation?, company?, claimant_user?, purpose/classification Identity/PublicTender/Compliance/Bank/Technical/Commercial/Fulfilment/Invoice/Contract/ImportSource/ExportResult, storage object/file reference, original safe filename, MIME, bytes, checksum, upload_intent, scan_state Quarantined/Clean/Rejected, scan metadata, issue_date?, expires_on?, expiry_instant?, parent_type/id/revision, retention_class, legal_hold?. Identity evidence is bound to a verified claimant and claim before organisation membership exists. PublicTender files require a company and staff publisher, not a vendor organisation. Vendor evidence requires its organisation and, for company-purpose evidence, company. Import/export evidence inherits initiating actor and explicit operation scope. Replacements are new evidence records; clean state is service-managed.

**PROC Authority Delegation:** company, original authority user/role, delegate user, permitted stages/actions, value ceiling/currency, valid_from/until, approving policy owner and reason, state Proposed/Active/Revoked/Expired. Delegated authority is bounded by the original grant and live conflict/independence checks.

**PROC Review Note:** company, application/submission, author, restricted reviewer scope, text and created_at. It is excluded from applicant DTOs, public comments and notifications; access follows the application assignment and internal-note predicate.

**PROC Review Decision:** company, application/submission, stage key, requirement/evidence revision?, actor, outcome Accept/Reject/More information, applicant_safe_reason, internal_note_reference?, observed_at, method/source, effective_until?, decision hash. Append-only; superseding decision references the old record. **PROC Review Task:** target, stage, assignee, due_at, delegation?, status Open/In progress/Completed/Cancelled/Overdue, decision?. Distinct-person stage slots are unique by submission/stage/actor.

**PROC Waiver:** company, organisation, application?, requirement key, scope/category, reason, evidence, proposer, independent approver?, expires_at, state Proposed/Approved/Rejected/Expired/Revoked. **PROC Remediation Campaign:** company, new requirement versions, affected categories, selection manifest, deadline, hold policy, published decision, operation. Generates linked remediation applications/tasks without rewriting old submissions.

**PROC Approval Decision:** company, target type/id/revision/hash, policy version, stage/slot, actor, decision Approve/Reject/Return, reason, delegation?, decided_at. **PROC Tender:** company, request, mode Public/Invited, draft revision, current_published_version?, closes_at, lifecycle Draft/Pending approval/Open/Closed/Technical evaluation/Commercial evaluation/Award decision/Awarded/Cancelled/No award, procurement owner, reissue_of?. **PROC Tender Version:** tender, sequence, hash, title/public summary, scope/evidence references, requirements versions, category/coverage/eligibility JSON, lots/price lines, bidding_currency, clarification_cutoff, briefing policy/date, closes_at, scoring criteria/maxima/weights/pass gates/quorum, technical/commercial combined weights, published ranking score precision/rounding, terms, publication approval/by/at. **PROC Tender Invitation:** company, tender, organisation, invited_version, match_reason, status Invited/Revoked/Responded, created_by/at; unique `(tender,organisation)`. **PROC Tender Participation:** tender, organisation, followed/first_download/submitted markers and latest acknowledged version, used for required notices; unique `(tender,organisation)`. ## Bids and evaluation

**PROC Bid Head:** company, tender, organisation, draft_revision, effective_version?, status Draft/Submitted/Withdrawn/Needs acknowledgement/Nonresponsive/Disqualified/Qualified/Awarded/Unsuccessful. Unique `(tender,organisation)`. No prices or unrestricted File attachments on this record.

**PROC Bid Draft Technical / PROC Bid Draft Commercial:** head, revision, bounded typed responses or price-line rows, evidence references, modified_by/at. Separate permissions even while editable. Commercial payload includes one `offer_valid_until:Datetime` instant; rows contain lot/price-line key, quantity/UOM, unit_rate, discount, tax, total, currency, delivery and permitted qualification/alternative labels. Native currency precision and server calculation apply.

**PROC Bid Version:** head, sequence, tender_version, submitted_by/at, technical_payload reference/hash, commercial_payload reference/hash, receipt_hash, supersedes?, withdrawn_at?, acknowledgement state. Unique `(head,sequence)`. Reaffirmation creates a new version/receipt bound to the current tender version and new envelope wrapper records referencing the same immutable content/checksums; it never rewrites an old envelope’s version reference. **PROC Technical Envelope / PROC Commercial Envelope:** immutable version reference, typed snapshot, evidence references, hash, released_at?, release_decision?. Generic document permissions deny all ordinary roles; application queries expose only permitted projections. Envelope content is excluded from standard Version/search/realtime indexing.

**PROC Opening Record:** company, tender, stage Technical/Commercial, proposed_by, confirmed_by, manifest of bid versions/hashes, technical qualification references for commercial opening, confirmed_at, content_hash. One successful opening per tender/stage; amendments are audited new evidence, not another unrestricted release.

**PROC Evaluation Assignment:** tender, lot/criterion, user, role Technical/Commercial/Moderator, conflict declaration/evidence, state Invited/Accepted/Recused/Revoked/Completed. **PROC Score Sheet:** assignment, bid_version, revision, criterion rows (key/max/score/reason/evidence), total, submitted_at?, supersedes?, status Draft/Submitted/Superseded. Unique `(assignment,bid_version,revision)`. Immutable submitted scores; at most one effective head per assignment/bid.

**PROC Evaluation Summary:** tender/lot, evaluation revision, effective score-sheet manifest/hash, moderated technical values/pass decisions, commercial totals/price scores, combined ranking/tie flags, recommendation and rationale, submitted_by/at. Recompute into a new revision if any source decision changes; never manually overwrite derived totals.

**PROC Offer Revision:** company, tender, original bid version/commercial hash, requested change type Validity extension/Arithmetic correction/Permitted clarification, reason, proposed offer_valid_until/schedule/diff, vendor response/actor/time, independent review, approved content hash, state Requested/Responded/Approved/Rejected/Withdrawn. Effective summaries/awards explicitly reference an approved revision without modifying sealed content.

**PROC Award:** company, source_request, sourcing_route Formal tender/Simplified RFQ/Direct or emergency, tender?, native_rfq?, sourcing_exception?, evaluation_summary?, organisation, bid_version?, offer_revision?, quotation?, currency/value, lot allocations, proposer, approval decisions, compliance/budget observations, conditions/deadline, acceptance/deadline, complaint_hold, state Proposed/Pending approval/Conditional/Offered/Accepted/Declined/Expired/Withdrawn/Contracted. Formal awards require tender/effective bid/evaluation references; RFQ awards require approved request, native RFQ and selected Supplier Quotation; direct/emergency awards require approved request and sourcing exception. Nonformal awards use request lines without fabricating tender/score records. Child allocations link their source lot/request line and quantity/value. **PROC Allocation Ledger:** owned non-financial reservation/consumption records linking request/award/contract line to draft/submitted native document, with unique source-action key; locked totals enforce bounds and reconcile to native cancellation. Never presents as a GL ledger.

**PROC Complaint:** company, tender/award, organisation, submitted_at, grounds/evidence, assigned independent reviewer, resolution/reason/time, state Open/Investigating/Resolved/Withdrawn and hold decision. Published company policy supplies submission window.

**PROC Retention Policy / Legal Hold:** company/purpose, period, disposition, owner/approval; hold target and scope/reason/release. **PROC Settings:** site readiness switches, queue/scanner/storage adapters, sender configuration and health references, pilot allowlists; secrets use protected storage. Company-specific values live in Company Policy, never an ambiguous site default overriding a company.

## State and consistency rules

Approval decisions are append-only. Only service commands may change state pointers or derived fields. Application Approved, Tender Awarded and Contract Closed never imply Supplier global enablement, financial posting or bank settlement. All terminal transitions retain reason/time/actor. Cancel/reject states release only the associated draft reservations after authoritative reconciliation.

Schema migrations add explicit uniqueness/indexes, backfill in bounded batches, and detect conflicts before enabling constraints. Head records enforce “one current version” portably rather than relying on partial indexes. Lock ordering is owned by [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md).

Index company/status/modified for operational lists; organisation/company for access; evidence parent/classification; tender/version/organisation for bids; assignee/state/due for review tasks; outbox/event state/next_attempt; contract/source keys for allocations; registry uniqueness for invoices; and recipient/read timestamp for notices. Large immutable payloads are never included in list projections. Capacity qualification must inspect query plans and prevent unbounded child-table loads.
